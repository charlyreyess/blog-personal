## The challenge

A conservation and earth sciences website needed a fresh look. Instead of presenting a single design, I wanted to offer **several visual directions** so the choice could be made with good judgment.

## The solution

I designed and built **five complete proposals**, each with its own palette, typography and composition, plus a **catalog** to compare them:

1. **Forest canopy and biodiversity:** *bento grid*, biomass metrics and a seed bank.
2. **Geological strata and soil science:** soil and rock layers with a mineral visual language.
3. **Hydrology and oceanography:** watersheds and wetlands with a water-monitoring dashboard.
4. **Arid ecosystems and xerophytic flora.**
5. **Biosphere and life sciences.**

![Proposal 01 — Forest canopy](/proyectos/propuestas-diseno-01.webp)

## Outcome

A browsable catalog that lets you compare the five proposals and decide the site's visual direction before building it.
