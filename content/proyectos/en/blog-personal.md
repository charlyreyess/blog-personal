## The challenge

I wanted my own fast, professional portfolio to show my projects to Spanish- and English-speaking clients, without paying for hosting or depending on third-party platforms.

## The solution

- **Next.js** with static pages: very fast loading and good SEO.
- **Bilingual (Spanish / English)** with per-language routes and `hreflang` links for search engines.
- **Projects in Markdown**, versioned on GitHub.
- **Contact form and newsletter** with Resend, protected against spam, and emails that follow the site's design.
- **Private reply page** for messages, using signed links that expire.
- **Continuous deployment** on Vercel with every `git push`.

## Outcome

An accessible, mobile-friendly site that scores 97 in performance and 100 in accessibility on Lighthouse, and is published for free.
