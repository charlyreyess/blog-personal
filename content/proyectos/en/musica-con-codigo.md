## The challenge

Explore how far you can go composing music **with code alone**, without samples or production software.

## The solution

A page that synthesizes a full track in real time with **Tone.js**:

- **Full song structure:** intro, verse, *build*, chorus, *breakdown*, final chorus and outro.
- **Synthesized instruments:** kick (MembraneSynth), cymbals (MetalSynth), snare and effects (NoiseSynth), bass, pads and melody.
- **Automated filters** that open and close to create tension and release.
- **WAV export** with *offline* rendering.

## Outcome

A demonstration of in-browser synthesis and music programming that produces a downloadable track.
