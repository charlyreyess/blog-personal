## The challenge

Computer lab staff managed students, teachers, printing and lab rules with an older tool (PrintSalas 1.2) that had fallen short: it lacked modules for equipment control, sanctions and communication with users.

## The solution

I evolved the system into **AdminSalas 2.0**, with a new main menu and new modules:

- **Administration:** lab rules and equipment catalog.
- **Records:** sanctions and active users, for both students and teachers.
- **Queries:** equipment access logs, sanctioned users and sanction history.
- **Message server:** notices to students and teachers, including the welcome message.
- Student import, printing and printer control, programs, groups and semester rollover.

## Outcome

An application with more than 30 management screens, a **Windows installer** in Spanish and English, and a LaTeX **manual appendix** documenting every new feature. It's the administrative counterpart of [AsisteSalas](/en/proyectos/asistesalas).
